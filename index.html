Designing next‑generation structures for advanced manufacturing.

The Multifunctional Design & Additive Manufacturing (MDAM) Lab develops design methods for Industry 4.0 — topology optimization, lattice and porous structures, and process-aware design — and extends them to multiphysics problems such as heat transfer, fluid flow and biomechanics.

Explore our research Publications

Research themes

All research

Topology optimization

Multiphysics and multi-objective methods that find the best layout of material — from brackets to heat exchangers and implants.

Lattice & porous structures

Strut, plate and TPMS lattices for lightweighting, energy absorption, bone scaffolds and heat transfer.

Thermal–fluid design

Heat sinks and heat exchangers that pair optimized topology with minimal-surface lattices for electronics cooling.

Process modelling & AI

Fast distortion and residual-stress models and data-driven surrogates, built into the design loop so parts print right.
