We started our lab seminar series with two talks:

- **Dr. Yun-Fei Fu** — *Smooth topological design using SEMDOT* (Smooth-Edged Material Distribution for Optimizing Topology), an algorithm that produces smooth boundaries that can be printed directly on AM machines.
- **Chinedu Ifediorah** — *Large-scale topology optimization*, a project to make high-resolution optimization practical through a better framework and a user-friendly interface.
