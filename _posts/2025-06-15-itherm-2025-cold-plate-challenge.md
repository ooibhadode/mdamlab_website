A joint team from the MDAM Lab and the MSAM Lab at the University of Waterloo won the **2024/2025 ASME K-16 & IEEE EPS Student Cold Plate Design Challenge** at ITherm 2025.

The winning design combined additive manufacturing and computational design for thermal management, and was a genuine collaboration between the Waterloo and Alberta teams.
