---
title: "New Mitacs partnership with Davey Textile Solutions"
date: 2025-09-15
---

We have started a Mitacs Accelerate research collaboration with [Davey Textile Solutions Inc.](https://daveytextiles.com/)

The project uses Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM) to develop ergonomic, efficient, application-specific tooling and fixtures for textile manufacturing. Along with solving real problems on the shop floor, the goal is a structured DfAM framework for low-volume, industry-specific tooling.
