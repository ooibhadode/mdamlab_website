Three lab members presented their work at the 2024 CSME/CFD Conference at the University of Toronto — on TPMS structures for heat transfer, large-scale topology optimization, and a new BCC-based lattice.

On the same trip we visited the MSAM lab at the University of Waterloo. A great few days of talks, conversations and, yes, the banquet.
