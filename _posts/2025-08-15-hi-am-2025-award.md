---
title: "Best Abstract award at HI-AM 2025"
date: 2025-08-15
---

Our work on an integrated computational framework for strut-, plate- and TPMS-type lattice design received the **2nd Place Best Abstract Award** at HI-AM 2025 in Waterloo.

The lab also presented new results on the thermo-hydraulic performance of uniform and hybrid TPMS heat exchangers, showing how combining Gyroid, Diamond and FRD structures can raise heat transfer while lowering pressure drop — promising for compact thermal systems in electronics cooling and energy applications.
