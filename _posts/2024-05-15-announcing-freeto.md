title: "Announcing FreeTO"
date: 2024-05-15
---

FreeTO (Freeform Topology Optimization) is an open-source MATLAB code for 3D topology optimization. It works on a structured hexahedral mesh and includes tools to initialize, optimize and post-process designs, producing smooth final geometry.

[Get FreeTO on GitHub](https://github.com/ooibhadode/FreeTO).
