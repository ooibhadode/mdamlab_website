Congratulations to **Chinedu Ifediorah** on completing his MSc in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Alberta.

His thesis, [*Topology optimization for additive manufacturing: porous structures and a human-centric framework*](https://ualberta.scholaris.ca/items/dfa24130-eff1-4336-a5f6-3733d032539a), advanced topology optimization of porous structures with graded porosity and self-supporting constraints, and led to [Optiworks](/software/#optiworks), a human-centric software framework for structural optimization. It is a good example of what we aim for: computational design that ends up in tools people can actually use. We wish him every success in his next chapter.
