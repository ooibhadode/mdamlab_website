Congratulations to **Chinedu Ifediorah** on completing his MSc in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Alberta.

Chinedu's work advanced high-resolution topology optimization and led to [Optiworks](/software/#optiworks), a human-centric software framework for structural optimization. It is a good example of what we aim for: computational design that ends up in tools people can actually use. We wish him every success in his next chapter.
