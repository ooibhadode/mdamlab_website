For the second year running, the joint MSAM–MDAM team is among the best in the ASME K-16 / IEEE EPS student cold plate competition. Our design was named one of three finalists in the **2025–2026 Cold Plate Student Design Competition**, out of 14 teams from around the world.

The team was **Joseph Nonso Orakwe** and **Xinhao Yin**, mentored by Prof. Ehsan Toyserkani (MSAM Lab, University of Waterloo) and Dr. Osezua Ibhadode. Experts from the ASME K-16 committee reviewed each team's design and white paper. Our design had the highest predicted figure of merit in the field, at 10.96%.

The three finalist designs are being printed by Fabric8Labs and tested at Georgia Tech, and the teams will present their work at IEEE ITherm 2026 in Orlando. See the [competition announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/asme-k-16_we-are-pleased-to-announce-the-finalists-activity-7444078789099413504-QGdn) for all finalists and honourable mentions.
