2025 was a milestone year for the lab: a run of journal and conference papers, student awards, new research grants, and our first two graduates.

None of it happens without the people in the lab and the academic and industry collaborators who support us. In 2026 we're building on that momentum with new collaborations, new student opportunities, and more work on structural design, optimization and additive manufacturing.
