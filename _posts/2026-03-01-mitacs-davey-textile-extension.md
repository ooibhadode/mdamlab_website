Our Mitacs Accelerate project with [Davey Textile Solutions Inc.](https://daveytextiles.com/) has been extended.

The project uses design for additive manufacturing (DfAM) to develop ergonomic, efficient tooling and fixtures built for textile manufacturing. The collaboration lets us work on real production problems while building a structured DfAM framework for low-volume, industry-specific tooling. The work is led by **Armin Hassanirad**.
