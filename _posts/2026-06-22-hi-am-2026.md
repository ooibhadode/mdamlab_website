date: 2026-06-22

Lab members attended the [HI-AM 2026 conference](https://hiam.uwaterloo.ca/2026/) on June 22–23, Canada's main academic meeting on additive manufacturing.

**Esosa Egharevba** presented a [poster](https://virtual.oxfordabstracts.com/event/75941/submission/86), and **Muhammad Irfan** attended as a co-author of a submitted conference paper. The meeting was a good chance to catch up on new research and industry work in AM and to meet researchers from across the country.
