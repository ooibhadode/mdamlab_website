This website uses Google Analytics to understand how people use it: which pages are visited, how visitors find the site, and roughly where they are (country or city). Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. We do not collect names, email addresses or other personal information through these cookies, and we use the data only to improve the site.

The data is processed by Google under its privacy policy. You can stop Google Analytics from collecting data about you by blocking cookies in your browser, or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

If you contact us or apply for a position, we use the information you send only to respond to you and to assess your application.
